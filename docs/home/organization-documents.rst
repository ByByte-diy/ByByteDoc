Organization Documents
======================

This page links to the organization-wide policies and community documents that
apply across the whole ByByte.DIY ecosystem. They are maintained in the
`ByByte-diy/.github <https://github.com/ByByte-diy/.github>`_ repository and serve
as the canonical reference for every project repository.

Core documents
--------------

* `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md <https://github.com/ByByte-diy/.github/blob/main/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md>`_
  — correct use of names, logos, and visual identity in educational, community,
  and commercial contexts.

* `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md <https://github.com/ByByte-diy/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>`_
  — expected behavior, reporting, and maintainer responsibilities for a
  welcoming community.

* `LICENSE.md <https://github.com/ByByte-diy/.github/blob/main/LICENSE.md>`_
  — canonical licensing policy for software, hardware, and documentation across
  the ecosystem.

* `TRADEMARK.md <https://github.com/ByByte-diy/.github/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md>`_
  — trademark policy: allowed and prohibited uses, forks, logo rules, and how
  open licenses relate to branding.
