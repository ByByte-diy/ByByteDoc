ByByte Nano Assembly Guide
==========================

This section describes the hardware assembly process of the ByByte Nano robot platform.

Before starting assembly, verify that all required components are available and that you have the necessary tools for soldering and mechanical assembly.

Overview
--------

The ByByte Nano platform is designed with a modular architecture. Users can build a minimal robot configuration and later expand it with additional sensors and modules.

The assembly process consists of four major stages:

#. Selecting a robot configuration.
#. Preparing all required components.
#. Understanding the hardware modules.
#. Assembling and testing the robot.

Assembly Difficulty
--------------------

+------------------------+------------------+
| Configuration          | Difficulty       |
+========================+==================+
| Basic                  | Beginner         |
+------------------------+------------------+
| Basic + Side Sensors   | Intermediate     |
+------------------------+------------------+
| Basic + Camera         | Intermediate     |
+------------------------+------------------+

Tools Required
--------------

* Soldering iron
* Solder wire
* Side cutters
* Pliers
* Small screwdrivers
* Utility knife
* Multimeter
* Tweezers (optional)
* PCB holder (optional)
* Heat-resistant mat (optional)
  
Safety Notes
------------

.. attention::

   Assembly requires soldering and the use of electronic tools.
   Children should perform assembly under adult supervision.

.. attention::

   Always disconnect the battery before modifying or servicing the robot.
